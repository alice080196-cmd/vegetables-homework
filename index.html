Vegetables

Carrots

A little crunch brings a lot of color to the table. Enjoy carrots sliced into a fresh salad, roasted until tender, or simmered in a warming soup.

Kitchen tip: keep them in the refrigerator and wash them just before cooking.

Sweet Corn

Golden kernels add a gentle sweetness to everyday meals. Steam a whole cob, grill it with a little butter, or stir the kernels into rice and soup.

Kitchen tip: choose fresh green husks and cook soon after buying for the sweetest flavor.

Bell Peppers

Bright, crisp, and easy to enjoy, bell peppers make a colorful addition to a salad or stir-fry. Roasting brings out their mellow, sweet flavor.

Kitchen tip: remove the stem and seeds, then cut into strips for a quick snack.
